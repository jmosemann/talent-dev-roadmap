## Roadmap Purpose

This roadmap is designed to help Honda Suzuki of Sanford build a stronger operating system supported by Herohub.

The goal is not simply to increase training activity. The goal is to create:

- stronger leadership ownership
- clearer decision-making authority
- improved department profitability
- consistent employee development
- repeatable onboarding systems
- reduced dependence on the Dealer Principal for daily operations

---

## Primary One-Year Success Definition

**Assessment response:**

> “To be able to take more time off and not worry about the day-to-day operations of the dealership.”

The roadmap therefore prioritizes owner independence, leadership accountability, and operating consistency.

---

# 2. Executive Summary

Honda Suzuki of Sanford has a strong foundation of dealership experience, OEM training exposure, and experienced employees. The assessment indicates the greatest opportunity is not simply adding training programs, but creating a more intentional operating structure where managers own outcomes and employees have clearer development paths.

The dealership’s stated priorities are:

1. Increase service department profitability
2. Increase F&I profitability
3. Increase parts department profitability
4. Improve technician efficiency
5. Improve hiring and training capability
